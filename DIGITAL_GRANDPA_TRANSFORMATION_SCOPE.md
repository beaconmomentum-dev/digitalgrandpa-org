# Digital Grandpa Public Foundation: Approved Transformation Scope

**Status:** Implementation blueprint. The content below replaces the current conversion-first, shared-property framing; it does not authorize a contributor archive, community membership, data transfer, or automated outreach.

## 1. Public role

Digital Grandpa is a **distinct public legacy-and-wisdom property**. It offers thoughtful field notes, consented stories, resource orientation, and intergenerational letters shaped by compassion, dignity, humility, and practical direction. It is neither a technology-training site for seniors nor a diagnosis, crisis, legal, financial, or clinical service.

The property is part of the Beacon family of independent work. Its public role is separate from Beacon Momentum, Beacon Community, and Beacon Labs. A visitor may choose to follow a clearly labeled referral to a Beacon destination, but a Digital Grandpa visit, inquiry, contributor submission, or future account does not create a Beacon account or transfer data automatically.

## 3. Public language and content rules

The public site must lead with grounded guidance rather than transformation promises. It may describe founder experience as personal perspective, but must not state unverified outcomes, “lives transformed,” ratings, download counts, client stories, treatment outcomes, employment outcomes, or statistical success claims.

Every future Field Note must state its basis: **Founder reflection**, **named public source**, **consented contributor story**, or **editorial synthesis**. Stories require documented permission, a human editorial review, attribution preference, removal path, and a record of any substantive editorial change. No person’s story becomes training material or marketing material by default.

## 4. Data, account, and service boundaries

Phase one has no email capture, checkout, webhook, payment, CRM sync, automatic referral, or contributor intake. It uses no third-party automation platform as a system of record.

Future forms may be considered only after a Digital Grandpa-specific privacy notice, minimal-data specification, retention schedule, consent record, support owner, export/deletion process, and approved implementation path exist. Any Beacon referral must use a direct link and its own destination-specific notice; no profile, email, story, or account data transfers automatically.

## 5. Design direction

The public foundation should retain a warm **blue/amber** relationship to the Beacon family while creating a visibly distinct Digital Grandpa atmosphere: editorial paper warmth, a porch-light/lantern accent, calm serif display typography, highly legible sans-serif body copy, and generous reading space. The site should feel like a respectful invitation to sit down and think—not a funnel, a clinical setting, or an application dashboard.

## 6. P0 removals

The implementation must remove or disable the following legacy elements from phase-one routes:

1. Unsourced testimonials, testimonial carousels, star ratings, downloads, “lives transformed” claims, and outcome metrics.
2. “Free resources” email capture, customer-data webhooks, and unapproved conversion/urgency components.
3. Legacy Rise & Reclaim conversion calls, shared membership language, and claims implying automatic cross-property movement.
4. Public payment, checkout, Stripe, HighLevel, PHP, Node, or email-queue interfaces not part of the approved phase-one scope.
5. High-consequence support claims without a qualified resource path and a clear non-emergency boundary.

## 7. Phase-one acceptance criteria

The public foundation is ready for release only when all of the following are true:

- Every published claim has an identified basis or has been removed.
- There is no public form, webhook, payment, CRM, tracking pixel, or invisible data transfer.
- Every route shares the same header, footer, accessibility patterns, support link, and explicit Beacon relationship language.
- Desktop and mobile views work without horizontal overflow or unreadable contrast.
- The `www` canonical route and HTTPS redirect behavior have been fixed or a documented deployment correction is ready.
- A human owner has approved the final content, referrals, and deployment path.
