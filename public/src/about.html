From Broken to Bridge

A journey of transformation that proves no fall is too far for redemption to reach

⚓
1981-1987

Navy Service & Divine Encounter

Served with distinction in the U.S. Navy. Experienced a profound supernatural encounter with the Holy Spirit during a conversation with the ship's Chaplain - a moment of literal weightlessness that would remain locked in his heart for decades.

🏆
1987-2003

Business Success & Recognition

Built Pinnacle Insurance Agency, expanded into investment banking, and received Operation Share's 1000 Points of Light award. Achieved the American dream by age 40, but pride led to a dangerous sense of self-sufficiency.

⚖️
2003

The Impossible Choice

Faced with an untenable choice: plead guilty to white collar crimes he didn't commit, or watch his family be destroyed while fighting from prison. As a father and husband, he chose to sacrifice his freedom for their future.

🙏
2003-2017

Prison: Breaking & Remaking

14 years in North Carolina's prison system became God's classroom. Served as chaplain's clerk, librarian, GED tutor, and teaching assistant. Learned that ministry isn't about position—it's about meeting people in their brokenness.

🏠
2017-2021

GraceHouse: Proving Transformation

Founded GraceHouse Transitional Ministries in Durham, NC. While state statistics show 66% of released inmates return to prison, GraceHouse graduates maintained a recidivism rate of less than 10%—proving transformation is possible.

🌐
2022-Present

Digital Grandpa: Global Mission Field

Launched Digital Grandpa as a mission field, not a ministry—serving 1,200+ souls worldwide regardless of religious background. In 2025, expanded with Rise & Reclaim Community, welcoming survivors, veterans, and freedom fighters.

The Starfish Principle

My life remains like that metaphorical starfish thrown back into life-giving waters, but now I understand my rescue wasn't just for my own salvation—it was so I could help throw others back into the waters of grace and purpose.

Digital Grandpa operates as a mission field, not a ministry. We welcome people of any faith or no faith at all, because spirituality is a deeply personal journey that transcends denominational boundaries.

Today, through Digital Grandpa and Rise & Reclaim Community, this movement continues to demonstrate that no fall is too far for redemptive power to reach, and that broken people, when touched by grace, become the very instruments of healing for others walking the same difficult path.

"Mission fields are real, while religions are man-made. Each serves as a control device over the communities they claim to serve. True transformation happens when we meet people where they are, not where we think they should be."

— Robert Burr
