Scope and care

Clear boundaries are part of respect.

Digital Grandpa is an educational and editorial public site. It is not an emergency, clinical, legal, financial, housing, employment, or crisis-support service.

Phase-one privacy posture

This public foundation does not offer a user account, email subscription, checkout, contributor submission, or public contact form. It does not automatically send visitor or story information to Beacon Momentum, Beacon Community, Beacon Labs, or another property.

Any future intake will require a property-specific privacy notice, purpose statement, minimal-data standard, consent record, retention period, export/removal path, and named human owner before it is enabled.

Inclusiveness and mutual respect

Digital Grandpa welcomes people of every background, appearance, identity, age, history, and life circumstance. The only shared expectation is mutual respect: treat other people as you would want to be treated. The property is not affiliated with a religious organization and is not a nonprofit organization.

Any future private community space will apply this standard consistently. Harassment, discrimination, exploitation, threats, or repeated disrespect may result in removal from that community space without refund. This rule will be stated plainly before any paid community offering exists.

Accessibility and site concerns

If a visitor encounters an accessibility barrier on this public site, they may write support@beaconmomentum.com with “Digital Grandpa site accessibility” in the subject. This support route handles website concerns only; it is not emergency or personal-case support.

Emergency boundary

If you are in immediate danger or considering self-harm, call or text 988 in the United States, call 911, or contact local emergency services. Digital Grandpa cannot monitor or respond to urgent personal circumstances.
