Scope and care

Clear boundaries are part of respect.

Digital Grandpa is an educational and editorial public site. It is not an emergency, clinical, legal, financial, housing, employment, or crisis-support service.

Reader access for The Work You Still Carry

Purchasers of The Work You Still Carry receive protected reader access after verified payment. The package includes the visual reader PDF, accessible-reader PDF, EPUB edition, ten chapter audio tracks, matching transcripts, optional reflection pages, and Digital Dignity and Choice cards.

If a file will not open, a download is incomplete, or you need the format list again, use the Digital Grandpa reader-access route below. Please include only the minimum information needed to locate an order or access record. Do not send payment-card details, passwords, account codes, or a personal story.

Get reader-access help Read reader policies

Purchase access is separate from optional updates. You can receive the files without agreeing to promotional messages. Digital Grandpa reader information is not automatically transferred to Beacon properties.

Privacy, refund review, and accessibility

Reader-policy details—including the 14-day human refund-review route, individual-use license, privacy treatment, and support boundary—appear on the Porch Light Library reader policies page and are linked before payment.

The package includes visual, accessible-reader, EPUB, audio, and transcript formats. If you encounter an accessibility barrier, use the reader-access route so a human can review the issue.

Inclusiveness and mutual respect

Digital Grandpa welcomes people of every background, appearance, identity, age, history, and life circumstance. The shared expectation is mutual respect: treat other people as you would want to be treated. The property is not affiliated with a religious organization and is not a nonprofit organization.

Harassment, discrimination, exploitation, threats, or repeated disrespect may result in removal from any future private community space without refund. This rule will be stated plainly before a paid community offering exists.

Site accessibility and non-reader concerns

If a visitor encounters a general website accessibility barrier, they may write support@beaconmomentum.com with “Digital Grandpa site accessibility” in the subject. This support route handles site and reader-access concerns only; it is not emergency or personal-case support.

Emergency boundary

If you are in immediate danger or considering self-harm, call or text 988 in the United States, call 911, or contact local emergency services. Digital Grandpa cannot monitor or respond to urgent personal circumstances.
