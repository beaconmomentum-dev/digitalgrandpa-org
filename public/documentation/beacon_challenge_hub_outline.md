# BEACON Challenge Hub - GoHighLevel Implementation Guide

## Overview

The BEACON Challenge Hub is a dedicated 30-day guided experience designed to help participants transform scattered energy into focused impact using the BEACON framework. This implementation guide outlines the structure, automation, and content needed for GoHighLevel deployment.

## Challenge Structure

### Week 1: Foundation & Assessment
**Days 1-7: Building Your BEACON Foundation**

Participants begin by understanding their current prioritization system and establishing baseline measurements. The week focuses on introducing the BEACON criteria through daily exercises and self-reflection activities.

### Week 2: Application & Scoring
**Days 8-14: Scoring Your Opportunities**

Participants learn to apply the BEACON framework to their current projects and opportunities. This week emphasizes practical application through guided scoring exercises and peer feedback sessions.

### Week 3: Decision & Focus
**Days 15-21: Making the Hard Choices**

Participants use their BEACON scores to make strategic decisions about which projects to pursue, pause, or abandon. This week includes decision-making frameworks and accountability partnerships.

### Week 4: Implementation & Momentum
**Days 22-30: Building Sustainable Systems**

The final week focuses on implementing chosen projects and establishing systems for ongoing BEACON application. Participants create action plans and commit to long-term practices.

- Success story sharing

**Daily Challenge Emails (30 emails)**
- Morning motivation and daily focus

- Peer connection facilitation
- Resource sharing and tool access

**Post-Challenge Follow-up (7 emails)**

- Results analysis and next steps

**Custom Fields for Participant Tracking**
