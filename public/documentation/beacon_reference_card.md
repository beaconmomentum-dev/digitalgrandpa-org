_**The BEACON Quick Reference Card**_

A one-page summary of the six criteria for ethical prioritization.

---

### **B**enefit

_How does this serve others before it serves me?_

*   **Focus:** Community impact, ethical considerations.
*   **Goal:** Ensure every action contributes positively to the intended audience.

### **E**ase

_Is this practically achievable with my current resources?_

*   **Focus:** Realistic assessment of time, budget, and skills.
*   **Goal:** Prevent overcommitment and ensure project feasibility.

### **A**lignment

_Does this align with my deepest values and mission?_

*   **Focus:** Core purpose, long-term vision.
*   **Goal:** Ensure every project is a step toward your true north.

### **C**ontinuity

_Can this be sustained without burning me out?_

*   **Focus:** Long-term viability, energy management.
*   **Goal:** Protect your well-being to ensure continued impact.

### **O**utcome

_What is the tangible, measurable impact of this work?_

*   **Focus:** Clear metrics, meaningful results.
*   **Goal:** Define success and track progress toward it.

### **N**otability

_Does this enhance my reputation for ethical, meaningful work?_

*   **Focus:** Credibility, signal value.
*   **Goal:** Build a legacy of trust and integrity.
