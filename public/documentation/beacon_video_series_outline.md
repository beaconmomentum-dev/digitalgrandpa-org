_**BEACON Mini-Video Series Outline**_

A six-part video series, with each video focusing on one of the BEACON criteria.

---

### Video 1: **B**enefit - The Heart of Ethical Impact

*   **Concept:** Introduce the "Benefit" criterion and its importance in ethical decision-making.
*   **Story:** Share a personal story of a project that lacked community benefit and ultimately failed.
*   **Action:** Guide viewers through a simple exercise to evaluate the benefit of one of their current projects.

### Video 2: **E**ase - The Power of Practicality

*   **Concept:** Explain the "Ease" criterion and how it prevents burnout and ensures project completion.
*   **Story:** Tell a story of taking on a project that was too complex and the resulting stress and failure.
*   **Action:** Provide a checklist for viewers to assess the practical ease of their projects.

### Video 3: **A**lignment - Your Personal North Star

*   **Concept:** Discuss the "Alignment" criterion and its role in maintaining focus and motivation.
*   **Story:** Share a story about a time when you pursued a project that was misaligned with your values and the resulting lack of fulfillment.
*   **Action:** Lead viewers in a short journaling exercise to identify their core values and mission.

### Video 4: **C**ontinuity - Building for the Long Haul

*   **Concept:** Explain the "Continuity" criterion and the importance of sustainable effort.
*   **Story:** Tell a story of a project that was successful in the short term but ultimately unsustainable.
*   **Action:** Guide viewers in creating a "sustainability plan" for one of their projects.

### Video 5: **O**utcome - Measuring What Matters

*   **Concept:** Discuss the "Outcome" criterion and the importance of defining and measuring success.
*   **Story:** Share a story of a project that felt busy but produced no meaningful results.
*   **Action:** Help viewers define clear, measurable outcomes for their projects.

### Video 6: **N**otability - The Signal of Your Work

*   **Concept:** Explain the "Notability" criterion and how it builds a reputation for ethical and impactful work.
*   **Story:** Tell a story of a project that not only produced great results but also enhanced your reputation and attracted new opportunities.
*   **Action:** Guide viewers in crafting a "notability statement" for their work.
