# BEACON Implementation Tools

## Interactive Pipeline Tracker

The BEACON Pipeline Tracker is designed to move ideas through a systematic evaluation process from initial intake to final retrospective analysis.

### Pipeline Stages

**Stage 1: Intake**
New ideas and opportunities enter the pipeline for initial consideration. This stage captures basic project information including description, timeline, and initial gut feeling assessment.

**Stage 2: BEACON Scoring**
Ideas receive systematic evaluation using the six BEACON criteria. Each criterion is scored on a 1-5 scale with detailed justification for scoring decisions.

**Stage 3: Decision Point**
Based on BEACON scores, ideas are categorized as Green Light (24+), Yellow Light (18-23), or Red Light (below 18). Decision rationale is documented for future reference.

**Stage 4: Implementation**
Approved projects move into active development with milestone tracking, resource allocation, and progress monitoring against original BEACON projections.

**Stage 5: Retrospective**
Completed projects undergo final analysis comparing actual outcomes to BEACON predictions, generating insights for future decision-making improvement.

## Fillable PDF Scorecard

The BEACON Scorecard provides a structured format for evaluating opportunities with automatic calculation features.

### Scorecard Structure

**Project Information Section**
- Project name and description
- Estimated timeline and resources required
- Key stakeholders and decision makers
- Initial assessment date

**BEACON Criteria Evaluation**
Each criterion includes a 1-5 scoring scale with descriptive anchors to guide consistent evaluation across different projects and evaluators.

**Automatic Calculation Features**
- Individual criterion scores sum to total BEACON score
- Color-coded results (Green/Yellow/Red) based on total score
- Percentage breakdown showing relative strength across criteria
- Recommendation engine suggesting next steps based on score ranges

## Industry-Specific Scorecards

### Non-Profit Organizations

**Benefit Criterion Adaptation**
Focus on community impact measurement, beneficiary reach, and alignment with organizational mission. Scoring emphasizes direct service delivery and measurable social outcomes.

**Ease Criterion Considerations**
Evaluation includes volunteer coordination complexity, regulatory compliance requirements, and funding acquisition difficulty specific to non-profit operations.

**Alignment Assessment**
Emphasis on mission alignment, board approval likelihood, and consistency with organizational values and strategic planning documents.

**Continuity Evaluation**
Focus on funding sustainability, volunteer retention, and program scalability within resource constraints typical of non-profit environments.

### Consulting Businesses

**Benefit Criterion Focus**
Client value creation, problem-solving effectiveness, and competitive differentiation in the marketplace receive primary emphasis in scoring decisions.

**Ease Assessment Parameters**
Evaluation considers existing expertise levels, client relationship requirements, and delivery complexity relative to current operational capacity.

**Alignment Considerations**
Personal brand consistency, expertise area focus, and long-term business strategy alignment guide scoring in this criterion area.

**Outcome Measurement**
Revenue generation potential, client satisfaction metrics, and referral generation likelihood receive detailed evaluation and scoring.

### Creative Professionals

**Benefit Evaluation**
Audience impact, creative expression value, and cultural contribution receive emphasis alongside traditional business benefit measurements.

**Ease Considerations**
Creative process complexity, technical skill requirements, and resource availability specific to creative work inform scoring decisions.

**Alignment Assessment**
Artistic vision consistency, personal creative goals, and brand identity alignment guide evaluation in this specialized context.

**Notability Focus**
Portfolio enhancement, industry recognition potential, and creative community impact receive heightened consideration in scoring.

### Startup Entrepreneurs

**Benefit Analysis**
Market need validation, customer problem-solving effectiveness, and competitive advantage creation guide benefit criterion evaluation.

**Ease Evaluation**
Technical feasibility, market entry barriers, and resource requirement assessment relative to startup constraints inform scoring.

**Outcome Projections**
Revenue potential, market size, and scalability prospects receive detailed analysis and scoring consideration.

**Notability Considerations**
Investor appeal, media attention potential, and industry disruption capability influence scoring in this criterion area.

Each industry-specific scorecard maintains the core BEACON framework while adapting evaluation criteria to reflect the unique challenges, opportunities, and success metrics relevant to specific professional contexts.
