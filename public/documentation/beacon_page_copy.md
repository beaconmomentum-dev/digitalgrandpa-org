## 3. What is BEACON?

BEACON isn't another productivity hack; it's a decision filter for ethical impact. It’s a systematic framework designed to transform your scattered energy into focused, meaningful action. It moves beyond 'what can you do' or 'what makes money' to 'what should you do'—creating meaningful change while ensuring your capacity for continued impact and preventing the very mistakes I made.

The framework is built on six interconnected criteria:

*   **Benefit:** How does this serve others before it serves me?
*   **Ease:** Is this practically achievable with my current resources?
*   **Alignment:** Does this align with my deepest values and mission?
*   **Continuity:** Can this be sustained without burning me out?
*   **Outcome:** What is the tangible, measurable impact of this work?
*   **Notability:** Does this enhance my reputation for ethical, meaningful work?
