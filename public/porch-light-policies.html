Reader policies in plain language.

These terms explain the product, payment, access, privacy, support, and refund-review route before purchase.

Last updated: October 13, 2026

What this package is

The Work You Still Carry is a $297 USD one-time, self-guided digital reader-and-listening package. It includes the reader editions, chapter audio, matching transcripts, private reflection pages, Digital Dignity and Choice cards, and protected reader access described on the sales page.

It is offered for individual self-guided use. It is not a technology course, business opportunity, certification, live cohort, personal coaching program, or individualized advice service.

What this package is not

Digital Grandpa does not provide emergency, clinical, legal, financial, tax, medical, benefit, security, housing, employment, or crisis support. The content is educational and reflective. It does not promise a particular personal, financial, business, work, or health outcome.

Individual-use license

Your purchase grants you a personal, non-transferable license to use the included materials for your own self-guided reading and listening. You may not resell, repost, redistribute, share access credentials, publish, or make commercial use of the files without written permission from Digital Grandpa.

14-day human refund review

If this package is not a good fit, you may request a refund review within 14 calendar days of purchase. We will review the request under the current policy and reply with the next step. Please do not send payment-card details, passwords, or account codes by email.

Refunds are not decided by an automated system. A chargeback, duplicate order, tax or invoice question, wrong-recipient delivery, suspected fraud, privacy concern, or missing entitlement is also routed for human review.

Privacy and data treatment

Digital Grandpa is a distinct property. Purchase or reader-support information is not automatically transferred to Beacon Momentum, Beacon Community, Beacon Labs, or another property for marketing or profile-building.

For a purchase, the payment processor receives the information needed to complete payment and issue a receipt. The protected reader-access system receives the minimum information needed to provide the purchased materials and resolve access issues. Digital Grandpa does not receive or store payment-card numbers.

If you separately choose optional Digital Grandpa updates, that consent is voluntary, unchecked by default, and separate from purchase access. Declining updates does not affect your purchase or ability to receive the package.

For reader-access help, please send only the minimum information needed to locate an order or access record. Do not send a personal story, passwords, payment-card details, or account codes.

Support and accessibility

The package includes a visual reader PDF, accessible-reader PDF, reflowable EPUB, narrated chapter audio, and matching transcripts. If a format will not open or an accessibility barrier prevents access, contact the Digital Grandpa support route. We will use the minimum information needed to help with file or entitlement access.

Request reader-access help

Questions or changes

For a privacy question, access issue, or request related to your purchase record, email support@beaconmomentum.com with “Digital Grandpa reader support” in the subject line. This is a reader-access route, not emergency support or personal counseling.
