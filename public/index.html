I learned something that changed everything

I'm an honorably discharged veteran who returned home to watch my community collapse. Factories closed, opportunities vanished, half the town moved away. So in the mid-90s, I took my family to North Carolina and rebuilt our lives from the ground up.

I became successful again. Counselor specializing in trauma recovery. Self-made millionaire. Received President Bush's 1000 Points of Light Award for community service.

But success made me a target. After 9/11, government agents saw a profit opportunity in my success and didn't care to learn the truth. They set out to destroy me with fictitious narratives designed to extract maximum financial gain through extortionate plea deals.

I chose to self-sacrifice to protect my family and allow them to go on without me, free from ongoing harassment and bullying.

Sometimes love means taking the hit so others can be free.

That wasn't defeat. That was the most important decision I've ever made.

— Bob

For over 8 years, I've been sharing the hard-won insights from that journey with veterans, formerly incarcerated individuals, and at-risk youth. Not theories from textbooks, but truths forged in real fire.
