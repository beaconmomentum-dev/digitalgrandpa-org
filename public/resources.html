A clear door

Resources without a runaround.

When a person needs more than a field note, a clear qualified resource is better than a vague promise.

If you are in immediate danger or considering self-harm, call or text 988 in the United States, call 911, or contact local emergency services. Digital Grandpa does not provide crisis intervention or emergency support.

How Digital Grandpa will use this page

Future resources will be labeled as a public source, a founder-created orientation note, or a separately identified Beacon referral. A referral never means that Digital Grandpa has sent your data or that another service has accepted responsibility for your situation.
