🎯 Analytics & Email Automation Setup

Complete setup instructions for Google Analytics 4 and GoHighLevel integration

📊 Google Analytics 4 Setup

Step 1: Create GA4 Property

  1. Go to Google Analytics
  2. Click "Create Account" or use existing account
  3. Create a new GA4 property for "DigitalGrandpa.org"
  4. Copy your Measurement ID (format: G-XXXXXXXXXX)

Step 2: Update Tracking ID

In script.js, replace the placeholder tracking ID:

analytics: { gaTrackingId: 'G-XXXXXXXXXX', // Replace with your actual GA4 tracking ID enabled: true, debug: false },

Step 3: Set Up Goals & Conversions

In GA4, create these conversion events:

  • email_signup - When users submit email form
  • rise_reclaim_click - When users click Rise & Reclaim links
  • resource_download - When users download resources
  • section_navigation - When users navigate between sections

📧 GoHighLevel Email Automation Setup

Step 1: Verify Webhook URL

The current webhook URL in the code is:

https://services.leadconnectorhq.com/hooks/xiByhBN0HkemBQ3gLZxB/webhook-trigger/710239a7-39fa-4367-b0e8-096ef4c0341c

Verify this is correct in your GHL account under Automations → Webhooks

Step 2: Test Webhook Reception

  1. Submit a test email through the website form
  2. Check GHL webhook logs for incoming data
  3. Verify the payload includes:
{ "firstName": "Test User", "email": "test@example.com", "tag": "digitalgrandpa-free-resource", "source": "digitalgrandpa_website", "veteran_detected": false, "timestamp": "2025-01-23T10:30:00.000Z" }

Step 3: Set Up Email Automation Flow

In GHL, create an automation triggered by the webhook:

  1. Trigger: Webhook received with tag "digitalgrandpa-free-resource"
  2. Action 1: Add contact to "Digital Grandpa Leads" list
  3. Action 2: Send welcome email with resource links
  4. Action 3: Tag contact based on veteran_detected field
  5. Action 4: Start nurture sequence (7-day email series)

Step 4: Create Email Templates

Set up these email templates in GHL:

Welcome Email (Immediate)

  • Subject: "🎁 Your Digital Grandpa Resources Are Ready"
  • Include download links for requested resources
  • Personal message from Bob
  • Link to Rise & Reclaim community

Follow-up Sequence (7 days)

  • Day 1: Welcome & resource delivery
  • Day 3: "How are you doing with the resources?"
  • Day 5: Success story from community member
  • Day 7: Invitation to join Rise & Reclaim

📈 Monitoring & Optimization

Key Metrics to Track

  • Email Conversion Rate: % of visitors who submit email
  • Rise & Reclaim CTR: % who click Rise & Reclaim links
  • Email Open Rate: % who open welcome emails
  • Resource Download Rate: % who actually download resources
  • Community Join Rate: % who join Rise & Reclaim after email signup

Optimization Opportunities

  • A/B test email modal headlines
  • Test different Rise & Reclaim CTA placements
  • Optimize email subject lines for better open rates
  • Segment email sequences by veteran status
  • Add exit-intent popups for additional conversions
