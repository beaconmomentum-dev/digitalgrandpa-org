The Three-Phase Journey

Each phase builds upon the last, creating a comprehensive transformation that prepares you to become a bridge for others

💔

Phase 1: Breaking

Weeks 1-3

The journey begins with honest acknowledgment of where you are and embracing the brokenness that precedes breakthrough. This phase creates the foundation for authentic transformation by surrendering the illusion of control.

Week 1: Acknowledging the Fall
Honest assessment of current situation without shame or judgment
Week 2: Embracing Brokenness
Understanding that brokenness precedes breakthrough
Week 3: Surrendering Control
Learning to let go of self-sufficiency and pride
🔨

Phase 2: Rebuilding

Weeks 4-10

The longest and most intensive phase, where you systematically rebuild your life on a solid foundation. This mirrors Robert's years of spiritual and personal reconstruction during his incarceration.

Week 4: Foundation Setting
Establishing core values and principles for your new life
Week 5: Identity Reconstruction
Discovering who you are beyond your failures and mistakes
Week 6: Purpose Discovery
Finding meaning and purpose in your pain and experiences
Week 7: Skill Development
Building practical life capabilities and competencies
Week 8: Relationship Restoration
Healing damaged connections and building healthy relationships
Week 9: Service Orientation
Learning to help others while continuing your own healing
Week 10: Spiritual Integration
Connecting with something greater than yourself
🌉

Phase 3: Bridging

Weeks 11-14

The culmination of your journey, where you prepare to become a bridge for others. This phase focuses on developing the skills and mindset needed to help others cross from brokenness to wholeness.

Week 11: Becoming a Bridge
Preparing to help others cross from brokenness to wholeness
Week 12: Mentorship Training
Learning to guide others effectively without judgment
Week 13: Community Integration
Finding your place in healthy, supportive community
Week 14: Graduation & Commitment
Pledging to help the next person on their journey

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is this program for? +
This program is for anyone who has experienced significant setbacks, failures, or life transitions and wants to transform their pain into purpose. Whether you're formerly incarcerated, a veteran, someone who has lost everything, or simply feeling broken by life's circumstances, this program provides a proven pathway to rebuilding and helping others.
Do I need to be religious to participate? +
No. While Robert's journey includes spiritual elements, this program operates as a "mission field, not a ministry." We welcome people of any faith or no faith at all. The focus is on practical transformation principles that work regardless of your religious background.
What if I can't afford the full program cost? +
We offer scholarship rates for those experiencing financial hardship. Additionally, we have sponsor programs where community members and organizations help fund scholarships. No one is turned away due to inability to pay.
Are the sessions in-person or virtual? +
We offer both in-person and virtual cohorts to accommodate different needs and locations. Virtual sessions use secure video conferencing and have proven just as effective as in-person meetings for building community and facilitating transformation.
What happens after I graduate? +
Graduates become part of our alumni network and are encouraged to help mentor new participants. Many go on to take our "Starfish Principle Mentorship" training to become certified facilitators. The goal is to create a community of bridges helping others cross over.
